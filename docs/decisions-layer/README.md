# IXO, the Decisions Layer for Agentic Finance

IXO evaluates the evidence behind a proposed financial action and records the determination under an accountable authority. Its Decisions engine connects the conditions for earning or spending money to the systems that authorize and move it.

This documentation explains the company vision, the Decisions product and its place beside payment protocols and risk services. Product descriptions include the intended design. The [status page](07-status-and-sources.md) distinguishes capabilities reported in production, pilot services, reference implementations and proposals. Research date: 9 September 2026.

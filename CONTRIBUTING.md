# Contributing

This repository is a proposal for interoperable standardization. Changes that alter wire format, verification behavior, or normative language should include a problem statement and threat-model impact, updated examples and conformance cases, backward-compatibility analysis, and a statement of upstream coordination needed.

Do not add organization-specific trust assumptions, private identifiers, or claims that a third-party standard has adopted this work. By contributing, you agree to license your contribution under the MIT License.
